# 🔎 RealityCheck

### What they claim vs. what the evidence says.

RealityCheck is an AI-powered web intelligence platform that helps users verify claims made by local businesses and places by comparing those claims against evidence collected from publicly available web sources.

Whether you are looking for a **PG, hotel, restaurant, coaching centre, gym, rental property, or other local business**, RealityCheck helps answer a simple question:

> **"Can I actually trust what this place claims?"**

Instead of relying on a single rating or a handful of reviews, RealityCheck gathers information from multiple sources, analyzes the evidence, identifies supporting and conflicting information, and presents the results in an easy-to-understand report.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

When people search for a place online, they usually see claims such as:

- ⭐ "Highly Rated"
- 🔐 "24/7 Security"
- 🏠 "Fully Furnished"
- 🧹 "Excellent Hygiene"
- 🚇 "5 Minutes From Metro"
- 💯 "Best Coaching Centre"
- 🍽️ "Authentic Food"
- 🏆 "Number #1 Rated"

But there is a problem.

### The internet contains both claims and experiences.

A business may describe itself one way on its website, while recent reviews, news articles, maps, or other sources may tell a completely different story.
